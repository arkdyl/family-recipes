Stuff we actually cook at home.

1. Classic Scrambled Eggs

Very tasty, for a school morning; when you have to go educate quickly :-]

Ingredients:

  • 3 fresh eggs
  • A small pat of butter
  • A pinch of salt and pepper

How do I prepare it?

- you have to: melt the butter on low heat. Crack the eggs straight into the pan and stir slowly. Take it off while it looks a bit wet, it finishes cooking on the plate.
